The EFF's large word list for passphrases
=========================================

Kemlock ships a copy of the Electronic Frontier Foundation's large word list in
core/data/eff_large_wordlist.txt, as it is published, and the extension makes passphrases from it.

The list is by the Electronic Frontier Foundation (https://www.eff.org/dice), and is licensed under
the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 United States licence (CC BY 3.0 US):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/

Copyright: Electronic Frontier Foundation (https://www.eff.org/dice). The list is used as it is, with
no change to its words.
